About Prada

Prada is an Italian public company specializing in the production of clothing, shoes and accessories, which owns the fashion house and brand of the same name. The headquarters is located in Milan. The Prada family owns 80% of the company's shares

History

The company was founded in 1913 by Mario Prada and his brother Martino. They opened a store in the Galleria Vittorio Emmanuel II in Milan selling suitcases, bags and other leather goods imported from England, as well as goods made of silver, crystal and tortoiseshell. In 1919, Prada became the official supplier of the Italian royal family, making the coat of arms of the House of Savoy part of the company logo. In 1958, Mario Prada's daughter, Louise, took over the management of the company.

Fashion

Prada shows are the pinnacle of intellectual minimalism and avant-garde, created by Miuccia Prada and Raf Simons. They take place as part of Milan Fashion Week and set the main trends.
